Questions

Frequently asked

How does booking work?

Send an inquiry with your event details. We follow up for a personalized consultation, then send a custom quote based on your style and needs.

How much does live engraving cost?

Every event is quoted individually, based on guest count, event length, the pieces you choose, personalization, and location.

Where do you offer live engraving?

Live events are in Tampa Bay and surrounding areas. Gifts and favors ship nationwide within the U.S.

How long does each engraving take?

Each piece is engraved on-site in just minutes while your event continues around you.

How many guests can you serve?

Live engraving works for gatherings of many sizes, from intimate celebrations to large events. Tell us your guest count and we will plan the experience around it.

What do you need at the venue?

Just access to an electrical outlet. We bring our full engraving setup.

How do deposits and reservations work?

A 50% deposit is due upfront to reserve your date. The remaining balance is due 2 weeks before your event.

What is your cancellation policy?

Your 50% deposit reserves your date and is non-refundable. You may reschedule once with at least 30 days’ notice, subject to availability, and your deposit transfers to the new date. The remaining balance is due 2 weeks before your event and is non-refundable after that point. If we ever need to cancel, you will receive a full refund.

Can I return a personalized item?

Because every engraved piece is made just for you, custom items are final sale. If there is a defect, shipping damage, or a personalization error, email us and we will make it right.
