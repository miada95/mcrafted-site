# M Crafted — Website Design & Development Brief

**Tagline:** Thoughtfully curated. Uniquely yours.  
**Brand descriptor:** Events · Design · Experiences

## 1. Project overview

M Crafted is a luxury personalized gifting and live engraving brand. It offers customized products, curated gift boxes, personalized event favors, and interactive live engraving experiences.

The website must serve two equally important purposes:

1. Generate inquiries and bookings for private and corporate live engraving events.
2. Operate as a fully functional online boutique for personalized products, ready-made gifts, and curated gift boxes.

Visitors should quickly understand what M Crafted offers and be able to choose between **booking an event** and **shopping gifts**.

## 2. Audience and reach

### Individual customers
- Brides and wedding parties
- Baby and bridal shower hosts
- Birthday and celebration shoppers
- People looking for personalized gifts
- Customers shopping for meaningful, customized presents

### Business customers
- Corporate event planners
- Marketing and brand activation teams
- Businesses purchasing employee appreciation gifts
- Companies seeking branded merchandise
- Businesses purchasing corporate gift boxes

### Geographic reach
- **Live events:** Tampa Bay and surrounding areas initially.
- **Online shopping:** Nationwide shipping within the United States.
- Support both local pickup and shipping.

## 3. Brand identity and visual direction

The visual direction is **Luxury Boutique meets Modern Editorial**.

Follow the existing M Crafted logo and Instagram branding. Sample the actual logo for final color values rather than introducing a new palette.

### Color direction
- Warm ivory and cream for primary backgrounds
- Champagne gold for accents, borders, and details
- Muted sage green for secondary elements
- Deep botanical or forest green for primary typography

### Typography and styling
- Elegant serif fonts for headings
- Clean, sophisticated fonts for body copy
- Delicate script used sparingly
- Generous white space
- Editorial-style product photography and lifestyle imagery
- Subtle animations and transitions
- Minimal botanical and gold details

**Avoid:** charcoal, black-heavy backgrounds, bright yellow gold, overly decorative layouts, and generic party-planning templates.

## 4. Website navigation and sitemap

Proposed navigation:
1. Home
2. Private & Corporate Events
3. Shop
4. Gift Boxes & Event Favors
5. Our Work / Gallery
6. About Us
7. Contact

Keep two prominent calls to action in the header:
- **BOOK AN EVENT**
- **SHOP NOW**

Both must be easy to access on desktop and mobile.

## 5. Homepage structure

### Section 1 — Hero / brand introduction
- M Crafted logo and tagline: “Thoughtfully curated. Uniquely yours.”
- Strong hero photograph or short video showing live engraving
- Brief explanation of the brand
- Calls to action: **Explore Experiences** and **Shop Personalized Gifts**

### Section 2 — Experience live engraving
- Explain the live engraving concept
- Show the engraving machine in action
- Show guests choosing products and watching them being personalized
- CTA: **Book Your Event**

### Section 3 — Shop our collections
Feature:
- Personalized keychains and bag tags
- Jewelry and accessories
- Personalized drinkware
- Ready-made gifts
- Custom event favors

### Section 4 — Curated gift boxes
- Showcase styled gift boxes
- Offer pre-designed gift boxes
- Introduce a Build Your Own Gift Box experience
- CTA: **Explore Gift Boxes**

### Section 5 — Events we serve
Feature weddings, bridal showers, baby showers, birthdays and celebrations, corporate events, brand activations, grand openings, and appreciation events.

### Section 6 — How it works
Explain the process from inquiry and consultation to product selection, personalization, and event-day execution.

### Section 7 — Our work
- Event photography
- Engraving videos
- Finished personalized products
- Customer experiences
- Instagram-style gallery

### Section 8 — Customer reviews
Display testimonials from event clients and online customers.

### Section 9 — Final calls to action
- “Planning an event? Let's create something memorable.”
- “Looking for something special? Explore our shop.”

## 6. Private and corporate events page

Explain the live engraving experience in detail, including:
- What live engraving is
- How the experience works
- Available products and engraving options
- Event types and examples
- Photography and videos
- Event packages and starting prices
- Custom quote option
- Frequently asked questions

### Event inquiry form
Collect:
- Name
- Email and phone
- Event type
- Event date
- Event location
- Estimated guest count
- Desired event duration
- Products of interest
- Personalization preferences
- Estimated budget
- Additional comments

The booking process should be inquiry-based, followed by a personalized consultation and custom quote. Display packages and starting prices while allowing custom quotes.

## 7. Ecommerce / online shop

The shop should be a complete ecommerce store with these categories:
- Personalized accessories
- Keychains and bag tags
- Jewelry
- Drinkware
- Ready-made gifts
- Event favors
- Gift boxes

### Personalization functionality
Customers should be able to:
- Enter names, initials, dates, and custom text
- Select available fonts
- Choose available colors and product variations
- Select design options and icons
- Add personalization instructions
- Review selections before checkout

Support both made-to-order and ready-to-ship inventory.

Personalization should be entered by the customer during the shopping/checkout flow. The exact implementation should be tested on mobile.

## 8. Gift box builder

Offer two ways to shop gift boxes.

### Option A — Pre-designed gift boxes
- Browse curated gift collections
- Select an occasion
- Choose a gift box design
- Add to cart

### Option B — Build Your Own Gift Box
- Select a box or packaging style
- Choose products
- Customize individual items
- Add a gift message
- Review the completed gift box
- Add to cart and checkout

Ask the designer to quote the gift box builder separately. It may require a dedicated ecommerce app or custom development.

## 9. Shipping and fulfillment

Support:
- Nationwide shipping within the United States
- Local pickup
- Shipping rates and delivery options
- Inventory tracking
- Made-to-order product lead times
- Ready-to-ship product availability
- Order confirmation emails
- Order status notifications

Make shipping, processing, returns, and personalization policies easy to find.

## 10. Platform recommendation

Shopify is the preferred platform to evaluate because of the ecommerce requirements: product personalization, inventory management, gift box customization, shipping, and checkout.

Compare Shopify with Squarespace and explain:
- Advantages and limitations
- Subscription and transaction costs
- Third-party app costs
- Customization requirements
- Ease of owner management
- Ability to scale

The priority is functionality, ease of management, and scalability without sacrificing the luxury visual identity.

## 11. Instagram and social proof

Include:
- Prominent Instagram integration and photo gallery

- Customer testimonials
- Event highlights
- Product reviews
- Customer-generated content, with permission

- Corporate gifting
- Custom gift boxes

Include:

- Google Analytics integration
- Google Search Console setup
- Mobile optimization
- Email marketing integration

## 13. Technical and functional requirements

The website must be:
- Fully responsive and mobile-first
- Easy to navigate
- Fast-loading and secure
- Easy for the owner to update
- Connected to ecommerce checkout
- Equipped with functional inquiry forms
- Integrated with shipping and local pickup
- Designed to support future growth

## 14. Deliverables requested from the designer

Request:
1. Initial website design mockups
2. Homepage design
3. Desktop and mobile layouts
4. Complete website development
5. Ecommerce configuration
6. Product personalization setup
7. Gift box customization functionality
8. Event inquiry forms
9. SEO configuration
10. Domain connection
11. Payment and shipping setup
12. Training on managing products, orders, and website content

Request a clear breakdown of:
- Initial design and development costs
- Platform subscription costs
- Third-party app costs
- Ongoing maintenance costs
- Additional custom development costs

The domain is already owned. Confirm how it will be connected and ensure the owner retains control of the domain, platform account, content, and integrations.

## 15. Acceptance demonstrations

Before approving the finished project, ask the designer to demonstrate these three complete customer journeys on a mobile device:

1. A customer purchasing a personalized keychain, entering personalization, and completing checkout.
2. A customer building a custom gift box, adding personalized products and a gift message, and checking out.
3. A customer submitting a live event inquiry with event details and product preferences.

Also verify that the owner can independently add products, update prices, upload photos, manage inventory, review orders, and fulfill orders.

## Overall vision

Create a luxury digital storefront that reflects the quality and thoughtfulness behind M Crafted. Customers should immediately understand what M Crafted offers, discover the live engraving experience, shop personalized products, create meaningful gifts, and easily inquire about their next event.

The website should feel like a luxury boutique brand—not a generic ecommerce store or event-planning template.

**M CRAFTED — Thoughtfully curated. Uniquely yours.**
